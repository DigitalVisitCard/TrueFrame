My Solution

Microscopic Pixel Signature Analysis

I have developed a detection system based on sub-pixel forensic pattern recognition. It identifies the invisible mathematical fingerprint that every AI image generator leaves behind.

  • 1

    AI generators leave fingerprints

    Every generative model introduces unique statistical patterns at the sub-pixel level — patterns invisible to the human eye but mathematically detectable.

  • 2

    PixelTrace extracts the signature

    My software performs a pixel-level forensic scan to extract these embedded patterns from any image or video frame.

  • 3

    Instant classification

    The system produces a real vs. synthetic confidence score in under one millisecond, enabling detection at platform scale.

  • 4

    Platform integration

    Designed as an embeddable API/SDK — insertable directly into upload pipelines, search indexers, and content moderation systems.
